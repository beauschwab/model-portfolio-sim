name: morning-review
description: Review the freshness and evidence of completed Workbench runs and identify operational exceptions.
version: 0.1.0
---

1. Read workspace state and recent jobs. Identify the as-of date and revision.
2. Inspect completed relevant KPI, risk, income and treasury manifests and bounded tables.
3. Separate fresh, stale, failed, queued and unavailable results. Do not launch missing calculations.
4. Explain only supplied metrics and approved thresholds. Research defaults are not approved limits.
5. Return a morning brief with freshness, exceptions, source citations and proposed next steps.

Stop when required results or policies are unavailable; identify the exact missing inputs.
