## What changed

Native continuation fitting verifies the actual computed decomposition and solve,
preserving the original matrix, cutoff and valid candidate coefficients. Invalid
or nonconverged candidates use bounded direct one-sided Jacobi repair. The shared
history owner preserves the historical caller's epsilon and wide/tall shapes, with
work and bytes checked before design allocation. Covariance and nonlinear callers
remain separate. See [continuation](../models/commercial-checked-least-squares.md)
and [history](../models/native-history-least-squares.md) contracts.

The new numerical ticket originally caused genuine capacity refusals. A paid
raw-law feature/scan census now bounds structurally varying columns without
deleting any actual feature, path, basis pass or source evaluation. Fixed unstopped
fee observations have an additional narrow proof; stopped fee payoffs and complete
loan/fee sources still execute and remain charged. Caps and tolerances are unchanged.
