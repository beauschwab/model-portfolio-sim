## Implemented behavior

Canonical legal coupon balances reconcile native cumulative entitlement and
actual collected cash at every credit clock. Recognized coupon lots remain
separate; no residual is removed with a tolerance or invented terminal payment.
Explicit performing draw tranches retain their own activation date, source,
purchase cost and original EIR. Reviewed provisions, chargeoffs and legal waivers
retain separate book, legal and modeled claims through complete recovery tails.

Finite exercise now supports explicit before/after-cash phases and reviewed
rights-only termination. Original stopped Physical acquisition cash must reconcile
to immutable original cost/yield before acquired coefficients can be published.
Current scenarios cannot replace this original proof.

Generated exercise uses supplied joint Gaussian/credit laws, training-only
quadratic continuation and fresh antithetic evaluation. A separate compact graph
tier applies the corresponding Pricing policy to separately supplied Physical
laws, retains paired Pricing CRN and locked original OAS, and regenerates native
accounting owners one at a time. It does not retain every expanded owner at once.
General generated OAS calibration and path-specific draw/review actions reject.

Observability guards prevent future cash or future retained-payment balances from
selecting current exercise. Future canonical coupon metadata stays hidden until
accrual or observed payment. Actual cash and partial earning require available
fixings, including inherited indexed balance dependencies. Broad analytical
indexed arithmetic keeps its separate contract.

Commercial cohort and acquired-library jobs preserve independent persisted replay.
Native retained-session evaluation uses coefficients without raw-source replay or
repricing. Standalone finite candidate journals join genuine native Physical
owners in one daily Sim at actual purchase dates. Issuer assets require the
exclusive amortized-cost security class; generic security marks and mixed owners
reject. Supplied opening provenance is not verified opening accounting evidence.

Prepared-source sharing follows the actor's actual worker-thread confinement.
The worker constructs and uses the session internally; channels carry raw/reply
values. Thread-local `Rc` preserves exact immutable source sharing through staged
constraint updates. No unsafe `Sync`, callback or financial tolerance change is
introduced. An exact-owner sharing/control test gates retention and release.

Commercial treasury independently replays reviewed complete-tail journals before
native closing capital and bounded daily FTP. Reviewed P&L enters once; modeled
economic LGD is not a second deduction. Regulatory eligibility, units, weights,
requirements and policy inputs remain supplied.

Durable routes retain original queue deadlines, raw/source/executable identities,
independent raw-source and persisted replay, final object-byte checks and SQL
cancellation/lease/token/revision/attempt fences. Historical schemas remain
readable. Additive execution requires compatible rebuilt artifacts and explicit
saved-library/session rebuilding.
