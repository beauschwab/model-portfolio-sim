## Findings worth recording

- The pre-existing independent derivative verifier (`derivative_verification`)
  did not reproduce the native journal for an at-market swap with exactly
  offsetting legs: the native owner emits floating-noise mark/VM intents
  (1e-16) that Python computes as exact zero, so intent counts differ. The
  exact-zero posting filter is the structural cause. Both oracles now drop
  intents whose postings are all within the 1e-9 comparison tolerance of zero
  and renumber the rest; the fixture also avoids identical legs.
- Dollar-offset assessment must compare clean cumulative changes (after-payment
  carrying differences) at accrual-free dates before the instrument's final
  settlement; summing dirty mark changes would count settled coupons as fair
  value change and fail a perfect hedge at maturity.
- Codex delegation was unavailable (model/sandbox errors), so the Python
  reference was written directly from the specification rather than by a
  second author.

## ASU 2025-09 elections (same day, later lane)

Scope: #35 stage 4 elections from the issued ASU 2025-09 text (FASB, November
2025; effective for public entities in fiscal years beginning after 15 December
2026, early adoption permitted, prospective under 815-20-65-7).

- Similar-risk groups: `group` relationships with per-member hypotheticals,
  55-23A(a) pro-rata instrument ratios or 55-23A(b) pairwise per-unit ratios
  under the reviewed band, similarity at every assessment, and 55-23D
  dedesignation as of the last similar date (or a supplied specific date) with
  the instrument changes after that date returned to earnings, later
  reclassifications reversed and the retained OCI released pro rata.
- Choose-your-rate debt: `choose_your_rate` exposures (liability interest,
  documented options), `rate_hypotheticals` with terms as of inception, and
  `rate_election` events with the final retrospective assessment on the prior
  rate, ineffective-period earnings recognition and the new hypothetical
  governing subsequent assessment and reclassification.
- Swap plus written option (815-20-25-88, later the same day): `written_option_position_id`
  names a written cash-settled European swaption of matching notional on every
  path node (live, then cash-settled expired); the combination is one
  designated instrument for clean value, cumulative change and cash-flow mark
  re-ownership, with the hypothetical derivative method required.
- All are additive and explicitly selected; results without elections are
  byte-identical (election-only fields are omitted). The dual-hedge election
  still refuses; nonfinancial components are out of scope.
- Forecast timing (2026-10-17): `delayed` and `period_probable_not_to_occur`
  statuses under a reviewed `forecast_timing` policy; hand values on the
  shared fixture (February period reclassified on 15 March; March period
  cancelled on 10 February releasing its 31/60 share).
- Forward-starting hedges (2026-10-17): accrual-free dates generalized to any
  date strictly inside no period, so a forecast issuance hedged by a forward
  swap is designated and assessed before the first period and released over
  the issued coupons after termination at issuance (hand values on the shared
  fixture; dates inside a period still reject).
- Jointly designated swaps (2026-10-17): `additional_instrument_position_ids`
  combine several mapped swaps into one hedging instrument (hand values: two
  identical swaps at half proportion each reproduce the single-swap ratio, OCI
  and reclassification, with each swap's marks split half to OCI).
- Partial-term fair-value hedges (2026-10-17): `hedged_term_end_ordinal` bounds
  the hedged benchmark cash flows and the frozen-basis amortization to an
  assumed maturity at a coupon period end (hand values on the shared fixture:
  the whole-swap instrument against a two-period item fails the band and the
  frozen basis amortizes to 1 March).
- Physically delivered written options (2026-10-17): the combination admits
  `expired_european` with a reviewed delivered underlying of matching notional;
  the delivered swap continues under the option's mapped position, so its
  marks are re-owned and its coupons settle carrying (hand values: closing OCI
  equals minus the delivered settlement minus the inception option value).
- Report-date capital (2026-10-17): `hedge-treasury-1` accepts an explicit
  `interim_report` with `as_of` at a hedge path node before the complete tail;
  rows carry the journal through that node and reconcile to its trial balance
  (native and engine cases with refusals at the tail, off-node dates and the
  unselected flag).
- Seasoned openings (2026-10-17): relationships designated before the
  derivative opening date carry a reviewed `opening` block; balances post once
  against opening equity, measurement continues from the derivative opening
  clean values. The fixture adds a December period paid on 2 January; the
  derivative owner's after-payment clean value carries a matured unpaid coupon
  in full, which the hedged-item formula excludes, so the fair-value instrument
  uses a zero pre-opening coupon and the cash-flow opening value carries it.
  The opening also carries the remaining designated scale and frozen basis/OCI
  tranches, which continue straight-line amortization and pro-rata release.
- Derivative closing capital (2026-10-17): `derivative-treasury-1` maps the
  standalone reviewed derivative journal (no hedge accounting) to the treasury
  bridge: per-instrument cash, gross value, margin, opening-equity and earnings
  rows through the complete tail or an explicit interim node, reconciled to that
  node's trial balance; the AOCI policy block is optional and, absent, the
  bridge emits no extended AOCI audit keys (the Python reference mirrors that
  branch). Evidence: native `derivative_treasury` 2 passed, engine
  `test_derivative_treasury.py` 17 passed (with the rebuilt release binary)
  and API `test_derivative_treasury_jobs.py` 6 passed; the hedge treasury
  engine (23) and API (5) suites were rerun after the optional-policy change.

## Shared daily journal (`saved-hedge-mixed-1`, 2026-10-17)

Formal hedge owners run in the existing dated daily Sim through the decision
`portfolio-model-ledger` binary. Findings while building it:

- The derivative source's structural replay bound (quadratic in the horizon)
  is about 5.7 million work units per run for the 92-day example, and the
  ledger requires at least one stress scenario besides baseline, so the
  92-day example cannot run twice within the 8-million joined cap. The fixture
  rebuilds the same hedge program on 10-day periods (30 days).
- Keying OCI by relationship and position left recognition (under the swap)
  and reclassification (under the forecast exposure) unnetted per key; hedge
  postings are now keyed per relationship.
- The fair-value basis does not reach zero at the loan's maturity without an
  explicit settlement event; the new reviewed `hedged_item_matured` reason
  derecognizes it on the principal date.
- Decision-crate builds fail on this machine inside the worktree because
  MSBuild exceeds the 260-character path limit; they ran with a short
  `CARGO_TARGET_DIR` and the binaries were copied back to the expected path.
