# Exact credit covariate fitting and projection

`credit-covariate-1` fits physical competing transition hazards to exact observed exposure segments and projects a frozen model onto explicitly supplied borrower and macro driver paths. It is additive: the constant-row [exact calibration](credit-calibration.md) and [interval-censored snapshot calibration](credit-snapshot-calibration.md) retain their schemas and economics. Monthly snapshot endpoints must not be relabeled as exact transition observations.

Rust owns `credit_covariate::fit(&CovariateFitRequest)` and `project(&CovariateProjectionRequest)`. Strict JSON contracts reject unknown fields. Source revisions, chronological splits, units, availability dates and assumption policies are required. There are no Python numerical callbacks or new dependencies. The [synthetic example](../../examples/models/credit-covariate-1.json) demonstrates mechanics and always remains ineligible for financial adoption.

## Observations and feature ownership

The fit declares physical measure, `exact_transition_times`, `observed` or `synthetic` data kind, population/model-policy identities, separate training/holdout source and split identities, and `train_start < train_end < holdout_end`. Training and holdout split identities must differ. All clocks use Gregorian ordinals plus seconds of day; elapsed exposure is exact seconds divided by 31,536,000 (ACT/365).

Each segment retains segment/account/source identity, start/end, a live origin, ending, ordered raw features, term level and feature availability time. Features are supplied and constant throughout that exact segment. Their availability must be at or before its start. Known changes require a `covariate_boundary` and an exact same-state continuation at the boundary; transition endings require a following contiguous segment to start in the known destination. Right censoring ends the account's observed path. Overlap, gaps presented as continuous observed paths, self transitions, absorbing-state reentry and a terminal covariate boundary reject. Left-truncated account entry is allowed; the likelihood conditions on the observed entry state and does not fit initial-state probabilities. Separate observation identities are required for genuinely disconnected observation episodes; callers must retain original borrower lineage in their source records.

Training uses `(train_start,train_end]` transition recognition, with exposure clipped to the training window. An event exactly at training end belongs to training once; an event later in a crossing segment does not enter training. Holdout uses the remaining exposure and events after training end. Because event times and constant covariates are known, splitting an exact segment at the chronological boundary does not invent an event. This differs from crossing interval-censored panels, which need their separately declared exclusion policy.

Columns declare name, role (`borrower` or `macro`), unit, raw lower/upper domain, frozen offset/positive scale and a reviewed scaling source. The transformation is `z=(raw-offset)/scale`. Column and term order are explicit; the first term is the zero-effect reference. These transformations are supplied assumptions and are never recomputed from holdout. Review provenance is not proof that an external data preparer avoided leakage; actual source-vintage controls and external scaler validation remain necessary. Revised future observations cannot masquerade as values available at segment start.

## Hazard model and exact likelihood

There are three live origins: performing, watch and delinquent. Each origin declares either a reviewed `fixed_row` or explicit `log_linear` outgoing edges to a different live state, default or prepaid. The diagonal and omitted edges are exactly zero. For an edge from origin `r` to destination `j`, term level `k` and features `x`,

`eta_rj = intercept_rj + sum(beta_rj * z(x)) + term_effect_rj,k`

`lambda_rj = exp(eta_rj)` in transitions per exposure year.

For each constant-feature live segment of duration `t`, its negative log likelihood is

`t * sum_j(lambda_rj) - event_indicator * log(lambda_r,event)`.

All competing edges share that origin's actual time at risk. A default/prepay event ends live exposure once. A right-censored segment contributes survival exposure and no event. No additional probability weighting, independent PD/prepay survival multiplication or hazard clipping occurs. The equivalent Poisson model uses mean `t*lambda` with an exposure offset; the reported absolute exact-path density excludes the event `log(t)` constants from that Poisson representation. The [eha primary documentation](https://www.stat.ethz.ch/CRAN/web/packages/eha/vignettes/tpchreg.html) explains the piecewise hazard/Poisson likelihood equivalence. The [Federal Reserve's detailed 2025 model descriptions](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2025-june-supervisory-stress-test-methodology-descriptions-supervisory-models.htm) provide a historical example of loan/borrower and macro conditioning; this implementation is not a reproduction of those supervisory models or a statement of current legal requirements.

Each intercept/slope/term parameter declares initial value, lower/upper box bounds, ridge weight and ridge center. The objective adds `0.5 * weight * (parameter-center)^2` only as explicitly supplied. Penalizing an intercept is therefore possible but never implicit. Fixed-width parameter boxes are supplied fixed coefficients. A conservative box envelope over every declared feature domain and term level must guarantee finite positive exponentials and a total row hazard at most the shared 100/year limit. Invalid envelopes reject before fitting; projection never clips an excessive hazard into an admitted domain.

Unexposed fitted origins reject and require an explicit reviewed fixed-row assumption. A fitted edge with no training events also rejects because its unconstrained unpenalized log-linear intercept has no finite MLE. A caller can instead declare a fixed-row assumption or remove that edge under its explicit model policy. This does not manufacture an empirical zero hazard. A training transition on an omitted/zero edge rejects; an impossible holdout event is retained as a diagnostic with null holdout log likelihood.

## Optimizer, identification and eligibility

The native deterministic optimizer uses analytic scores and unpenalized information, a box-projected Newton direction, a bounded backtracking sufficient-decrease search, and a gradient fallback when the reduced information solve is singular or fails to descend. Ridge enters the objective/score and Newton system. It does not enter the information used to establish identification. Explicit iteration/backtrack, projected-gradient, relative-rank and timeout policies are retained. A bounded symmetric Jacobi eigensolver reports the unpenalized eigenvalues; unresolved residuals reject rather than inventing a rank.

Results retain convergence reason, projected-gradient norm, iterations/evaluations/work, active bound parameters, information rank/free dimension and eligibility reasons. Nonconverged, rank-deficient and active-bound estimates are numerically ineligible. Synthetic fits are always adoption-ineligible. An `observed` declaration plus numerical eligibility admits the projection interface; it is not proof of actual data provenance, forecasting performance or model approval. Reviewed fixed rows remain visibly supplied assumptions even if other rows have observed evidence. Observed bank histories and independent out-of-time review are still required for an empirical accuracy claim.

Training and holdout diagnostics report exposure, transitions, expected event counts (integrated compensators), complete-path log likelihood, impossible exits and censored segment counts. These expected counts are not horizon PDs. Holdout rows enter no objective, derivative, information calculation, scaling estimate or coefficient selection. Conditional independence and noninformative censoring given declared features are modeling assumptions. Frailty, borrower clustering, competing-exit dependence beyond the supplied state/covariates, selection effects and parameter uncertainty are not fitted by this contract. Information rank is a numerical diagnostic; the module does not output cluster-robust standard errors or confidence intervals.

## Immutable coefficient identity

Canonical training cases are ordered by account, clipped start time and segment identity. Their payload retains source IDs, clipped training start/end seconds, event only when recognized in training, known raw/transformed features, availability seconds, term and censoring information. Raw crossing-spell tail/end/holdout events are excluded. The training SHA-256 digest also includes training source/split/window, population/model policy, declared columns/terms/rows and optimizer policy. Holdout source/split/end/records are excluded.

The coefficient identity hashes the normalized typed `FittedModel` serialization with its own hash field empty. The model contains the training digest, fitted coefficients/bounds/ridge policies, source/feature identities and eligibility status. Changing only a crossing spell's later holdout tail/event preserves the entire fitted model byte for byte. Reordering input segments also preserves identity. Full-request publication identity must separately hash and retain holdout inputs/diagnostics. SHA-256 follows [NIST FIPS 180-4](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/FIPS/NIST.FIPS.180-4.pdf), with independent standard vectors in tests. The digest detects changed model content; it is not a signature or authentication/empirical approval credential. Projection validates the typed model domains and checksum before evaluating it.

## Supplied driver projection and shared random numbers

Projection embeds the fitted model, explicit immutable model revision and review identity, physical measure, position/scenario identity, start/end clock, timeout and complete driver intervals. Each interval retains an ID, exact dates, availability time, source revision, kind (`supplied_observed` or `supplied_projected`), ordered raw columns and term level. Intervals must partition the requested horizon without gaps/overlap and remain within declared feature domains. No macro forecast, missing history, interpolation or flat tail is inferred.

`adoption` rejects an ineligible fitted model. Explicit `analytical_only` can project a diagnostic/synthetic model but always returns `adoption_output=false`; production consumers must preserve that gate. Outputs are physical `3x5` annual hazard matrices, with ACT/365 `end_year` relative to the projection origin and interval/source identity. They can fill `credit_path::PathInterval.hazards_per_year`; coupon/accrual rates, LGD, CCF and full recovery tails remain independently supplied contracts. Existing draw-stream IDs, seeds/path keys and ordered random pairs are unchanged. Integrated event thresholds carry across projected term boundaries, so changed hazards alter economic timing without redrawing at a term change. There is no physical-to-pricing conversion and no automatically inferred joint macro stochastic process.

## Bounds and verification

Admission allows at most 50,000 exact segments, eight columns, 32 term levels, 24 declared parameters, 256 iterations, 64 backtracks, 20 million counted work units, 32 MiB charged metadata and 32 MiB canonical identity payloads. Individual labels are at most 256 control-free bytes. Fit windows are bounded to 200 ACT/365 years; projection to 100 years and 4,096 intervals. Parameter bounds lie within [-100,100], transformed feature domains within [-1e6,1e6], ridge weights within [0,1e6], and the hazard envelope guards exponentiation. Declared worst-case objective/backtracking/linear solve/eigensolver/diagnostic/hash work is admitted before optimization, then actual work is charged during execution. `std::Instant` deadlines are checked during admission, case evaluation, iteration/information and hashing. Process cancellation/blocked writes still require host transport controls. These component limits do not claim an aggregate process RSS cap or measured whole-book acceptance.

Independent fixtures cover events/exposure MLE, two-group log hazard ratio, analytic score/information against direct equations and central differences, rank deficiency despite ridge, bound and iteration eligibility, exact train-end counting, clipped holdout invariance, impossible holdout exits, availability/domain/unknown-field rejection, source order stability, checksum mismatch, worst-case work admission, exact covariate continuation, and macro projections into the existing shared-draw path engine. The synthetic example has four account-years at feature zero with one default and four at feature one with two defaults: intercept `log(0.25)`, slope `log(2)`, hazards 0.25 and 0.50/year. It supplies zero rows for unobserved watch/delinquent origins and contains no real bank data. This stage does not close source acquisition, empirical held-out validation, full macro-integrated production adoption or tracker #25 acceptance.
