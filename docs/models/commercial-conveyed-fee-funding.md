# Conveyed commercial fee funding and closing capital

These additive mathematical helpers retain purchased fee rights separately from
loan principal, loan effective interest and the seller's historical fee income.
They require explicit reviewed policies. A helper result does not verify a
source journal, authorize a regulatory method or establish empirical accuracy.
The finite candidate coordinator explicitly selects this inventory with
`secondary_fee_review` on `commercial-candidate-fee-treasury-1`. It requires an
actual complete-origin secondary source and the supplied review identity;
selection without that source or conveyed rights without selection rejects.
Historical omission retains the source-channels-3 route and its exact result.

## Funding inventories

`modeled-product-ftp-source-channels-4` retains the existing 24 fee funding lanes
and appends two nonnegative gross magnitudes:

| Channel | Native debit-sign balance | Typical reviewed asset-funding coefficient |
|---|---|---|
| `acquired_fee_asset_valuation_adjustment` | Negative contra asset | -1 |
| `acquired_fee_liability_valuation_adjustment` | Positive contra liability | +1 |

The source adapter must derive these amounts from native purchase and later
release postings. It must not copy the seller's cumulative service counters into
buyer income or silently net the adjustment into another gross channel. Channel
coefficients, their review identities and the treatment of negative funding are
supplied explicitly; the table does not install a policy automatically.

A receivable of six acquired for five retains a gross claim of six and a contra
asset of one. Its net funding balance is five. Settlement produces cash of six
and releases one of buyer purchase income, with no new service revenue. The
signed mirror retains a payable of six and contra liability of one: liabilities
are five and later buyer expense is one. A positive contra liability never
becomes an asset merely because its debit-sign balance is positive.

Every daily state retains all 26 channels and source origins. Once-only cash
assignments and reviewed noncash reallocations must reproduce every closing
state through the complete tail. Zero allocations still validate the entire
unweighted source. The result schema is
`modeled-product-ftp-preparation-proof-result-4`.

The selected component retains the existing 64-day, 128-origins-per-state,
65-reset, 4,096-event, 2-million-work and 1-MiB input/output limits. The two extra
lanes are charged by the same work formula. Component bounds are not aggregate
process-memory guarantees. Historical schemas 1, 2 and 3 do not accept this new
inventory or change their arithmetic or receipts.

## Closing capital

The private reviewed helper uses capability 9. Public credit-ledger selection
rejects it; it is not a new public journal protocol. The coordinator must first
verify the original source and ordered journal before supplying closing balances.

Fee assets and their negative asset adjustment form one separate fee-asset
exposure group per instrument/account/scenario. All constituent fee asset GLs
require the same explicitly supplied reviewed risk weight. Loan instrument
weights cannot assign fee weights. This prevents a standalone negative contra
group from being clipped to zero while leaving the gross purchased claim fully
weighted. Different constituent weights reject pending a richer reviewed
allocation contract. Gross fee liabilities, including their positive adjustment,
remain separate from assets and reduce liabilities once.

The existing reviewed negative-exposure policy, capital eligibility inputs,
thirteen ratios and minimum/buffer/management distinctions remain in force.
Neither purchase discounts nor modeled economic loss imply an extra automatic
capital deduction. The helper's additional
`conveyed_fee_asset_adjustment_grouped=true` flag describes its mathematical
grouping only.

## Validation and remaining adoption

The coordinator replays the same native loan/fee candidate journal before
constructing funding states or closing capital. Each daily checkpoint retains
all 26 channels and their origins, including zero participation. Purchase cash
and subsequent earnings come from actual native postings; cumulative seller
service counters never enter buyer income. The additional two channels and
origins are charged before generation within the unchanged 2M bridge-work and
128-origin limits. The 32-MiB result and separate 64-MiB JSON ownership phase
remain bounded independently of the financial source owners.

Capital uses private reviewed capability9 after ordered-journal replay. The
complete-tail closing date, entity, currency and scenario must match the supplied
policies; independent replay reconciles gross closing statements and asset/contra
groups before and after persisted publication. This is complete-tail closing
capital, not capital at an arbitrary earlier report date.

The existing Treasury adapter admits nonnegative asset funding. A negative fee
obligation after loan principal settlement can produce negative funding; the
adapter rejects it. An explicit reviewed channel policy may exclude that funding
credit while retaining every gross liability and buyer expense. No implicit
zero clipping is permitted. Standalone source4 signed research proofs remain a
separate contract and do not automatically qualify as Treasury positions.

The example `examples/models/commercial-conveyed-fee-treasury-1.json` is supplied
synthetic policy, not an approved regulatory or accounting methodology. Its
receivable6/cost5 acquisition retains contra1, separate purchase cash and buyer
income1, and no inherited seller service income.

Native hand controls cover both obligation directions, zero/half/full allocation,
complete origins, once-only cash/income, reviewed negative funding, cancellation,
asset/contra grouping, missing or unequal weights and rejection by historical or
public capability selectors. Finite candidate source/journal/treasury controls
also cover both signs, zero/half/full participation, explicit reviews and weights,
rehashed source/income tampering and final SQL/source/object/lease/deadline fences.
Saved/cohort/mixed-book integration, generated or partial secondary acquisition,
arbitrary report-date capital, broader reviewed
policies, observed evidence and complete issue 15/25 scale acceptance remain
required. Matching native artifacts must be rebuilt before selecting additive
schemas; local validation does not deploy or rebuild saved economics.
