# Independent mixed Card2 gross-rights verification

`analytics.product_rights_reference.verify(raw, result, check=lambda: False)` is
an independent publication verifier. It does not execute production finance or
call Rust, pricers or a discount backend. The raw selector `card_v2` chooses the
optional extension of `product-mixed-product-credit-1`. Old consumer/Card1
verifiers and saved economics remain unchanged.

The verifier reconstructs raw performing dated backbook/candidate sources and
raw three-class card histories, their original-yield daily income, atomic basis
transfers, reviewed default dispositions and actual reviewed interest cash.
It then schedules acquisitions, dated recognition, atomic product events and
dated settlement into one chronological pooled GL. A complete atomic group's
Event lines post once, in source order; shared intents are checked evidence and
never post again. Face allocation and physical path weight multiply actual
monetary postings once. Gross accrued rights and live/default signed interest
basis remain separate, including after principal reaches zero.

Full unweighted closure is required before scaling, even for zero allocation or
zero weight. Default claims with gross outstanding rights or signed basis reject;
net carrying value zero is insufficient. Original effective interest and reviewed
basis realization retain separate P&L. Future purchases have no opening source
state and acquire on their actual date. Reporting clamps each source to its
supplied purchase/full calendar; statement indices retain actual dates and do not
repeat after the source end.

Complete source, range, lineage, journal, trial, daily, reporting and closing
tables are compared. Date/index/range/work identities are strict integers;
financial comparison remains 1e-9 absolute plus 1e-12 relative. Cancellation is
checked throughout admission, generation and ordered comparison. Native Core2
allocator-specific retained charge is admitted only as metadata: the independent
full escaped payload lower bound and native declared reservation upper bound
must both hold. It cannot substitute for any financial source calculation.
Aggregate 128 MiB/8 million work and the primitive's own raw/capacity/action
limits are independently checked before projection. A conservative 1080-day
reservation is distinct from the actual complete replay window.

Successful Card2 receipts include `card_v2_rights_owned:true` and
`capital_ftp_eligible:false` and the reviewed `closing_capital_eligible:true`
permission. Complete-tail closing capital uses separately reviewed supplied
policies; this does not authorize interim/default capital or modeled FTP.
The native optional FTP scalar and each supplemental
row must explicitly retain false eligibility. This proves complete reviewed
accounting/source replay in the admitted physical lane; it does not prove
empirical accuracy, accounting-standard approval, joint stochastic adoption,
pricing credit adoption, interim default capital or modeled FTP eligibility.
