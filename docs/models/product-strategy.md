# Native actual-purchase product allocation

`strategy-product-1` adopts `product-units-1` through a distinct native allocation contract. It does not relabel a product result as `aged-units-1`. Rust prepares product histories, original-yield income, aged fixed-purchase-OAS risk, constraints and allocation replay. The existing C++ HiGHS implementation solves the linear program. Python/API code transports inputs and verifies publication independently.

## Financial source and units

The raw `product` is a complete `ProductUnitsRequest`. Only native preparation constructs `product_strategy::Prepared`; its fields are private, and it cannot deserialize supplied coefficients. It retains all original raw inputs and complete purchase/income/age receipts. `source()` exposes the immutable native `ProductUnitsResult`; `units()` exposes ordered explicit unit identities.

Allocation `face` is nonnegative actual face in `currency_units`, matching preparation. Every unit must appear once in exact prepared order, including zero allocations. Unit identity retains the true unit and template IDs, exact purchase date and reporting opening period, asset/liability side, accounting category, capital-policy revision and original cash quote per face. The decision solver's internal unique key is the unit ID; it is never reported as the original template ID.

Preparation preserves exact dense `[scenario, unit, 10 metrics, reporting period]` coefficients. Metric order is `nii, balance, dv01, hqla_l2a, outflow30, asf, rsf, rwa, assets, liabilities`. NII is the reviewed original-effective-yield amount on admitted physical performing cash; contractual coupon recognition and cash interest remain distinct. Physical/pricing measures and supplied paired pricing histories remain separate. Every aged mark retains original purchase OAS. No allocation evaluation refits yield or OAS.

The existing product preparation boundary remains: admitted performing repayment/runoff paths only, explicit original-yield policy and independently reconciled physical cash/purchase quote plus original locked-Q purchase price. Default, draws, nonaccrual, migrations, modifications and fee transitions require separate adoption. This solver stage adds no empirical fitting or stochastic history generation.

## Objective, funding and reviewed limits

The objective remains the existing worst-case **external** NII: maximize the minimum over supplied scenario baseline NII plus summed incremental effective-interest coefficients. Baselines accept normalized native `Base` fields or the existing saved KPI representation, converted by Rust. The supplied baseline and cash resource are assumptions; this route does not independently recreate a saved mixed backbook or generate an actual-date backbook liquidity path.

Funding keeps native `[S,N,T]` demand, with the union of actual purchase/report/cash dates and every complete cash tail beyond the reporting horizon. Each scenario/date row uses that scenario's physical cash. Gross pretax end-of-day cash netting follows the preparation policy. Already-weighted source events are multiplied only by allocated face, never by physical weights a second time. Cash interest is funding cash; original-yield interest is objective income. They are not substituted for one another.

`constraints.max_total_assets` caps new asset **face**. The separate `max_purchase_cost_assets` caps asset face times actual purchase quote. Liability issuance cash belongs to funding and is not an asset purchase. Existing LCR, NSFR, EVE and horizon CET1 formulas and thresholds are retained, with supplied research weights. Optional face limits require a label, reviewed policy ID, explicit selector (`unit`, `template`, `accounting_category`, `all_assets`, `all_liabilities`), sense (`<=` or `>=`) and nonnegative amount. Category/template amounts aggregate gross selected face; there is no net asset/liability cancellation. Unmatched selectors reject. Legacy commercial selectors must be explicitly mapped to these typed limits before this route executes.

Explicit capital rows retain their supplied eligible numerator/denominator deltas and ordered internal unit identities. A raw `capital_dependency` must equal the complete immutable product request exactly whenever capital rows are present. A change of input, model, grid, histories, quote, category or policy requires refreshed dependency and compatible rows (`CAPITAL_REFRESH_REQUIRED` on mismatch). Economic credit loss is not inferred as a regulatory capital deduction.

## Native interfaces and session transactions

Risk-native exposes `product_strategy::prepare(&ProductUnitsRequest)`, `Prepared::{units,source}`, and `evaluate(&Prepared,&[Allocation])`. Evaluation consumes immutable coefficients and independently replays physical source cash per scenario. It emits all ten selected metric amounts, source cash dates/components, cumulative funding demand, category/template face and separate purchase-cost totals.

Decision-native exposes `product_library::optimize(&Value)`, `PreparedLibrary::{new,preparation,snapshot,evaluate,solve}`, and `Session::{new,call}`. The request schema is `strategy-product-1`, operation `optimize_product`, with `product`, ordered scenario `bases`, `constraints`, `face_limits`, optional `max_purchase_cost_assets` and optional `capital_dependency`. No coefficient/exposure/cash overrides are admitted.

Session creation uses `create_product` at the shared actor boundary. Calls require the current integer version: `status`, `eval` with complete ordered allocation, `solve`, `update_product`, `publish`, `abort`. A staged update receives a monotonic token; the old snapshot remains authoritative until explicit publication. Abort discards it. Stale versions/tokens and a second concurrent pending update reject. Constraint/base/limit-only updates share immutable native preparation without a graph or income calculation. Raw unit edits rebuild changed units while retaining exactly equal native sources at unchanged ordered unit indices. Shared identity, currency, monetary unit, base scenario, reporting grid or unit-count changes force a complete rebuild. Equality covers every unit field, history, quote, yield, policy and age branch; it uses complete serialized identity without hashes as an equality substitute. Complete candidate admission still applies, and full-tail funding is reconstructed in its original stable source reduction order. Selected `eval` remains coefficient-only and does not invoke HiGHS. `solve` uses retained coefficients and HiGHS on its owning thread.

Every staged update returns its complete candidate `product_preparation` receipt before publication, including candidate raw source, tensors and native source lineage. Independent verification can inspect the candidate while `status` still exposes the original published snapshot. Malformed candidates leave no pending transaction. Constraint-only updates return the identical immutable source receipt.

Retained `eval` and `solve` do not copy the complete source receipt into each
response. Independent verification combines their result with the published
`status.snapshot.preparation` from the same session version; evaluation never
reconstructs that source or executes a financial pricer.

Durable `POST /models/runs` with `strategy-product-1` invokes the bounded standalone `portfolio-strategy` process. It retains separate queued-envelope and executed-raw hashes, executable/source identity and the original publication deadline. Independent Python verification reconstructs admitted performing consumer, card, fixed-mortgage and deposit sources, original-yield income, age marks, full-tail funding, ordered allocated amounts and every constraint before storage and after persisted Parquet decoding. This source/feasibility receipt does not certify global optimality, empirical calibration or a daily candidate journal. The public `strategy.product.ProductSession` provides the native staged interface; its session publication requires the caller to inspect and verify the candidate token.

Every solved or selected allocation undergoes existing native limit/KPI acceptance and additional source/metric amount replay. Source cash independently reconstructs funding at every full-tail date, and scenario NII/funding are compared with retained solver evaluation using unchanged `1e-12` relative plus `1e-9` absolute financial reference tolerance. Existing optimizer objective, row/KPI/capital/cash feasibility tolerances are unchanged. Infeasible/unbounded solves return an explicit non-feasible result; failed replay returns no validated result. `dynamic_validated=false` remains explicit: no mixed candidate daily journal acceptance is implied.

## Admission and evidence

All original product preparation work/input/retention bounds apply (256 units, 13 scenarios, 120 reporting periods, one million coefficient/funding values, 250,000 source cash events and 32 million preparation work units). Face limits are bounded to 100 and capital rows to 2,048. Existing HiGHS admits bounded dense work and a bounded solve time. Allocation amounts and reduced money must stay finite within the shared monetary domain. Cancellation checkpoints cover preparation, scenarios and retained cash-tail replay. A pending full rebuild retains old and new preparations; session and transport copies are additional to component retention, not an aggregate RSS guarantee.

Hand fixtures independently compute deposit premium EIR expense, liability issuance/maturity cash, installment effective interest from dated discount/accretion recurrence, face/cost cap separation, reviewed category and capital limits, exact source identity rejection and transactional update behavior. Two explicitly declared physical scenarios prove independent full-tail cash replay without cross-scenario doubling. These synthetic examples prove mechanics within admitted scope, not observed predictive accuracy, accounting-standard compliance, legal policy, saved-book integration or full 60,000-position joint graph/credit/strategy/journal acceptance. Those issue #23/#25 gates remain open.

`examples/models/strategy-product-1.json` supplies two otherwise identical installment cash schedules (529 on day 29, 515.5 on day 60), independently reviewed original yields 2% and 3%, and flat unit discount factors. Original locked spreads are respectively `log1p(.02)` and `log1p(.03)`, so Q cash quotes and P original yield reconcile without refitting. Each quote is `529 exp(-log1p(y)29/365)+515.5 exp(-log1p(y)60/365)`. A 100 face asset cap, a reviewed first-unit minimum of 20, and second-unit cap of 80 produce unique optimal face allocation 20/80 because the second unit earns greater incremental original-yield income. Hand effective interest is `I1=q expm1(log1p(y)29/365)` and `I2=(q+I1-529) expm1(log1p(y)31/365)`; objective is supplied baseline 20 plus each unit's `(I1+I2) face/1000`. Final total funding cash is `104.45` minus purchase cash. This fixture declares no empirical or scale evidence.
