## Ownership and exact replay

`SequentialCommercialCut` prepares the completed source, runs every original
baseline/named scenario through the cut with output muted, and moves all54 owned
`Sim` fields into an opaque native state. Exhaustive destructuring has no rest
pattern: a new simulator field requires an ownership review at compilation.
The state has no serialization, deserialization, cloning or public constructor.
All11 old borrows end before revised graph preparation begins. No monetary
checkpoint is reconstructed from JSON, and no acquisition is replayed.

Comparison evidence is a single bounded immutable encoding of the complete
retained specification, source/fee binding identities and ordered source groups
through the cut in every run. Revised evidence streams directly against those
bytes without another encoding or a parsed comparison Value. Original dates,
native clocks, signed zero, source policy identities, unweighted rights, allocation
and native group order remain significant. Appended exclusive AC owners must
start after the cut and acquire once. Complete future contractual and recovery
tails remain owned by the revised source.

After equality succeeds the proof bytes are dropped. Actual moved state attaches
to the revised owner and resumes at cut+1. Protocol9 completion records
`sequential_source_owners:true`; protocol8 and historical public routes retain
their previous behavior. Native predecessor verification remains false.

## Validation and outstanding adoption

Native controls compare every decoded output table with the historical dual-owner
cut across multiple cuts, zero/half/full allocations, future acquisition and signed
fee rights. Drop/lifetime controls establish that the completed owner is destroyed
before resume. Observed cash, clock, policy, signed-zero, lot and allocation changes
reject before visible output. The original cancellation authority and absolute
deadline remain effective between source phases.

`commercial_sequential_cut_reference` independently recomputes both preparations,
every source proof and complete cut journal/closing tables. Its frozen full-request
fixture is compressed losslessly and synthetic. Native model/controller callbacks
are forbidden in the Python controls. Mutation controls reject changed source
coverage, money, paths, raw history, graph coefficients, journal, lineage, closing,
completion types/fields and forged admission/publication flags. Schema1 diagnostic
witnesses remain readable. Resource fields remain reported metadata; the verifier
always returns `complete_resource_proof:false` and `publication_eligible:false`.

Required next steps are complete joint pre-Sim resource admission from raw input,
an independently derived resource census, bounded complete output transport,
successful unchanged positive API execution, independent persisted replay and
publication mutation controls, and exact verified predecessor binding. Only after
that positive case succeeds may dedicated refusal coverage move to a distinct
genuinely over-budget request. The original positive test must not be weakened.

All remaining #15/#25 gates in the implementation plan still apply, including
broader saved-book/cohort/strategy/product accounting and FTP adoption, observed
holdouts and complete mixed-book scale acceptance. Synthetic validation does not
establish observed accuracy, reviewed policy approval or deployment. No API/worker
restart or saved-library rebuild is implied by these local component checks.
