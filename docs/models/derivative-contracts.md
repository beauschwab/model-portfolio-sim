# Explicit derivative contracts and collateral paths

`derivative-contract-1` with original `derivative-contracts-1` or explicitly selected `derivative-contracts-2` adds an opt-in Rust analysis component. It does not reinterpret historical swap/swaption economics or close enhancement issue #20 by itself. The full production graph, saved hedge mapping, daily journal, FTP, scenario calibration and scale/publication gates remain separate integration work.

The protocol envelope is `{schema:"derivative-contract-1",threads:1,request:{schema:"derivative-contract-1",model_version:"derivative-contracts-1",operation:{kind:"swap"|"european_swaption"|"netting",request:{...}}}}`. Public native functions are `derivative_contract::evaluate`, `swaps::value_swap`, `options::value_european`, and `csa::evaluate`. Modules separate clean swaps, European contracts, collateral types, raw-trade handoff, and the collateral timeline. Python transports requests/results; it is not a financial backend.

## Clean actual-date swaps

Fixed and floating legs have independently supplied frequencies, accrual/payment dates and day counts, with common contractual start/maturity and constant nonnegative notional. There is no notional exchange. Floating coupons use the shared `term_enhancements::indexed` contract: explicit term, daily simple or daily compounded observations, index/calendar identities, reset/selection cutoffs, shifts, floors/caps and spread treatment. Observed fixings retain publication/revision identity. Conditional projections retain model/path/measure identity and their information timestamps cannot follow the exact UTC valuation cutoff. Arithmetic does not verify externally supplied calendar mappings or generate future forwards.

The collateralized discount curve is separate from the index basis and projected rate path. It carries an immutable source/revision, collateral-discount policy, currency, exact asof, information timestamp, and exact positive discount factors for every admitted payment date. The asof factor is exactly one. Intraday information must be available by the supplied valuation cutoff. Interpolation and extrapolation are rejected. Rates and factors need not be positive; discount factors must be positive, allowing negative interest-rate environments. Shared coupon-factor domains still apply.

For fixed coupons `N k tau_i` and floating amounts `F_j`, `A=sum(N tau_i D_i)`, `PV_fixed=k A`, `PV_float=sum(F_j D_j)`. Payer clean value is `PV_float-PV_fixed`; receiver clean value is its negative. Par rate is `PV_float/A` when `A>0`, otherwise unavailable. Signed dated cashflows independently reconcile the clean value. Payment timing explicitly includes or excludes coupons on the valuation date. Paid coupons are removed once; unsettled coupons retain their contractual accrual intervals. These pricing schedules are not daily accounting journal instructions.

## European cash or physical settlement

A European contract supplies a complete set of terminal conditional states, risk-neutral probabilities summing to one, asof-to-expiry discount factors and source identities, conditional-model/exercise-policy identities, and CRN identity. Every state revalues the raw underlying swap at expiry. The strike is the underlying fixed rate; payer/receiver is its contractual side. Contractual leg descriptors must agree across states. Forward/index information may be known at the terminal node, never after that node's exact valuation cutoff. No volatility surface, empirical tree, stochastic path, or exercise boundary is inferred.

Terminal intrinsic value is `max(clean_underlying_at_expiry,0)`. Long option value is `sum(probability * expiry_discount * intrinsic)`; short is its negative. Zero intrinsic expires. Cash settlement supports the **collateralized swap-value policy**: terminal cash equals intrinsic divided by the supplied expiry-to-settlement collateral discount factor. Thus delayed cash carries the same expiry PV. This is an explicit policy, not a claim to reproduce every ISDA settlement matrix or par-yield/realized-annuity convention.

Physical settlement at expiry emits only the exercised, mapped underlying future legs, weighted by the supplied conditional probabilities. It emits no intrinsic cash payment. Delivered position and legal netting-set IDs are mandatory. The output legs remain Pricing projections; a physical observed election or realized cash settlement requires separately supplied evidence and integration. Bermudan/American exercise is unsupported.

## Raw exposure and European expiry handoff

Each CSA scenario/date node contains every raw mapped trade and uses exactly the same clean swap/option pricers. Quoted exposures or sensitivity-only substitutes are not accepted. Native admission checks contractual descriptors across nodes, exact scenario/currency/time, payment timing and complete maturity/settlement tails.

A pre-expiry option is `Trade::EuropeanSwaption`. At expiry it becomes `Trade::ExpiredEuropean` with one complete, immutable supplied terminal state, an election source/revision, and the same option mapping slot. The expiry node is mandatory when crossed during the run. The selected state and election cannot change subsequently.

For physical exercise, the handoff carries the raw delivered swap with its mandatory delivered position ID, unchanged contractual legs/notional/strike, and the declared netting-set ID. Short physical options reverse the delivered swap side. Clean exposure is replaced by the current raw underlying swap value; the original option is not valued a second time. Unexercised options cannot deliver a swap. For cash settlement, no underlying is allowed; the outstanding cash claim uses its exact current collateral discount curve and disappears according to explicit before/after settlement timing. A cash discount route on physical delivery is rejected.

## Legal buckets, margin and collateral conservation

Every trade maps explicitly to entity, counterparty, currency, contract revision, netting set and reviewed legal identity. `enforceable_netting=true` permits signed aggregation within that reviewed bucket. Otherwise each trade remains an independent bucket, even if netting-set labels match. Mixed legal reviews in one bucket fail. Opening collateral cannot be guessed across multiple buckets: supply separate requests or zero opening balances.

VM is signed cash collateral: positive means received, negative means posted. Received and posted segregated IM are separate nonnegative balances. Static opening IM is a supplied reviewed policy input; this component does not calibrate or dynamically recompute IM. Segregated received IM is not reusable for funding and is excluded from unsecured exposure until the reviewed default waterfall applies it. Received VM reuse for funding is an explicit boolean.

VM target is `V-receive_threshold` above that threshold, `V+post_threshold` below the negative post threshold, and zero otherwise. Calls equal target less settled VM and outstanding pending calls. MTA, calendar-day settlement lag, exact due date and per-node settlement availability are explicit. Outstanding calls prevent duplicate calls. An outage leaves due calls pending until an admitted available node. Collateral remuneration settles in cash using each interval's opening balances, supplied signed rate and day count; it does not silently capitalize or convert IM into VM. Received-IM interest and returns are recorded separately as custodian cash.

Complete tails require final collateral return or explicit default closeout. Tail return cancels remaining open calls and returns VM/IM; unavailable final settlement fails. At each bucket, opening stock plus every signed collateral movement must equal residual stock. Default cancels unsettled calls before margin settlement on the default date. No business-day calendar, collateral securities, FX, cross-currency setoff, collateral rehypothecation right or legal opinion is inferred.

## Default waterfall and separate clean/XVA output

Only counterparty default is supported. Default date, closeout date, legal/replacement-value policy, unsecured recovery, posted-VM return recovery, posted-IM return recovery, posted-collateral setoff and received-IM availability are explicit. Default/closeout nodes and complete tails are mandatory. Closeout revalues raw trades at the closeout node.

Original v1 requests remain readable and executable under their prior supported scope. Selecting `default.mpor` requires `model_version="derivative-contracts-2"`; v1 with that new policy fails with an explicit selection/rebuild error. Without the reviewed policy, the original strict-intermediate-payment/exercise rejection remains. Neither version infers legal early termination or own-default/DVA mechanics.

### Native unpaid obligations and exercise during MPOR (v2)

`DefaultPolicy.mpor` is a reviewed `MporPolicy` with its own identity/revision, `claim_measure` equal to the path measure, `carry_policy="nominal_no_interest"`, and `exercise_rights_policy="preserve_contractual_european_to_closeout"`. Nominal no-overdue-interest carry and preservation of exercise rights are explicit legal/contract choices, not inferred universal bankruptcy rules. Overdue interest, penalty accrual, pre-default arrears, alternative termination/exercise rules and own-default remain outside this policy.

Every coupon, exercise and settlement date within default-to-closeout must be an admitted raw-trade node. Each cash event is identified by mapped `position_id`, native `event_id` and `payment_ordinal`. Event IDs are `fixed:<period_id>`, `floating:<period_id>`, and `option_cash:<selected_state_id>`. Instructions contain **no cash amount**: they declare either `retain_to_closeout`, or `settle_on_node` with an admitted due-or-later date and settlement-evidence identity. Settlement requires declared availability and occurs once before that node's closeout waterfall. Missing, duplicate, unmatched or unused instructions fail. A failed settlement must remain an explicitly retained claim; the engine does not invent a receipt from the scheduled date.

The native pricer reconstructs due cash from the mapped dated contracts. Fixed cash follows notional/rate/day count; indexed cash uses the supplied reset/observation arithmetic. For Pricing paths, captured claims retain the Pricing rate-source identity. For Physical paths, Pricing projections cannot be relabeled: actual coupon arithmetic must use observed fixings or a supplied `physical_index_paths` entry containing **index observations, source revisions and a Physical path identity**, with unchanged mapped coupon terms. Physical conditional observations must explicitly declare Physical measure and information available at the due-node cutoff. Publication/reset/coverage/calendar domains still apply. Physical cash-settled option amounts are contractual calculations on the reviewed single-state expiry clean value; retained election, exercise and discount identities disclose that valuation basis rather than claiming an observed market transaction.

Signed unpaid receipts and obligations survive contractual maturity and are retained through closeout. At a Before-payments node, due Pricing cash embedded in the raw clean mark is subtracted from remaining replacement value. The captured measure-specific claim is then added once, unless an explicit settlement instruction paid it. At an After-payments node, the raw clean mark already excludes the due cash. Default-day After timing excludes payments processed before default; default-day Before permits their capture. This preserves the distinction even when Physical fixing amounts differ from Pricing projections.

Exposure and closeout output explicitly partition `clean_value`, `replacement_after_due_obligations`, `unpaid_obligation_claim`, and `total_closeout_claim`. The waterfall receives only remaining replacement plus unpaid signed claims. `PathResult.obligations` retains native amount, dates, contract/source/measure, disposition, actual settlement date or inclusion in closeout. After closeout, neither old contractual rights nor unpaid claims generate a second exposure or cash payment.

European exercise inside MPOR uses the existing single-state native handoff under the declared rights-preservation policy. `mpor_exercises` records the reviewed election, selected state, calculated Pricing intrinsic, delivery identity and netting mapping. Cash exercise creates only the contractual settlement claim; physical exercise transfers the underlying rights once and creates **no intrinsic cash payment**. Subsequent delivered-swap coupons can become unpaid obligations under the original option mapping slot, retaining delivered position and election identity. Legal bucket rules govern whether signed obligations offset across trades; no netting is inferred from matching dates.

The complete native event/observation work remains bounded at 250000 across raw pricing and MPOR capture, with aggregate 32 MiB immutable metadata admission. Compiled maps avoid repeated linear instruction/date searches. Full maturity, coupon, option-settlement and recovery/closeout tails remain required.

For positive replacement value, received VM is applied first, then legally available segregated received IM; the residual is an unsecured counterparty claim. Excess received VM is returned once; excess received IM is returned by the custodian. Posted collateral return claims use their own supplied recovery fractions. Received VM already held is not emitted again as closeout cash. For negative replacement value, reviewed posted-collateral setoff reduces the obligation; unused posted collateral has separate return recoveries. All remaining posted/received stocks are closed once. Subsequent exposure rights are extinguished.

Pricing paths produce separate `clean_value`, discounted counterparty/posted-return credit loss (`cva`), and a supplied-spread funding adjustment (`fva`). Funding cost is the left-endpoint settled posted VM plus posted IM, less explicitly reusable received VM, multiplied by supplied signed spread, exact year fraction and endpoint discount. This is a disclosed deterministic interval convention, not a market-calibrated total-XVA model. Own DVA, dynamic IM/MVA, nonlinear funding and capital/KVA are unavailable. Physical scenario paths retain modeled movements and losses but do not become risk-neutral CVA/FVA values. No allowance, chargeoff, accounting designation, GL posting or regulatory-capital consequence is derived.

## Bounds, identity and compatibility

Actual `currency_units` are mandatory; currencies are three uppercase letters. Monetary values are finite and bounded by the shared `1e15` domain. At most 4096 periods per leg, 8192 exact discount points, 256 European states, 256 legal trades, 256 scenario paths and 4096 nodes per path are admitted. Combined raw-leg/observation work is at most 250000 per derivative operation. Direct swap, option and CSA immutable raw metadata is capped at 32 MiB; identities are control-free and at most 128 bytes. Date domains are at most 36600 days. These component bounds are not an aggregate process RSS guarantee.

Model/schema changes require explicit input/cache identity rebuilds. Contract, index basis, path, collateral discount policy/source, exercise model/policy, CRN, legal review, opening state, scenario and valuation ordering are preserved in typed requests/results. Integrators must include the complete immutable request in graph/cache identity, preserve fixed base OAS and shared CRN, retain full final-date tails, and independently verify persisted journals. No legacy default, existing saved economics or Python fallback changes are made here.

## Independent examples and checks

`examples/models/derivative-contract-1.json`: N=360, ACT/360 over ten days, fixed 5%, indexed 10%, DF=1. Fixed cash is -0.5, floating cash +1; payer clean value=0.5 and par rate=10%. Principal exchange is zero.

`derivative-option-1.json`: equal terminal weights, expiry DF=.99. Payer underlying at 10% has value=.5; at 2% has value=-.3. Long European value=`.5*.99*.5=.2475`. Only the positive state settles. The fixtures separately verify delayed cash carry and physical future-leg conservation.

`derivative-mpor-2.json`: default on January 3, contractual coupon on January 11, closeout on January 12. Native fixed cash -0.5 and floating cash +1 remain unpaid after maturity. Remaining replacement value is zero; unpaid claim is 0.5. With supplied 40% recovery, closeout cash is 0.2 and credit loss is 0.3. V1 policy selection rejects; explicit v2 succeeds.

`derivative-netting-1.json`: the same payer swap calls +.5 VM immediately and returns it when coupons settle. Net margin cash/stock changes are zero; clean=.5, credit loss=0 and supplied funding cost=0. Other independent tests cover unequal leg frequencies, exact intraday information, negative coupons/remuneration, lag/outage/pending calls, gross margin without legal netting, both closeout directions, segregated IM, complete-tail admission and European expiry replacement.

## Primary sources and scope

- [ISDA 2021 Interest Rate Derivatives Definitions](https://www.isda.org/2021/10/04/2021-isda-interest-rate-derivatives-definitions/) distinguishes confirmed economic terms and conventions. This component requires explicit terms rather than inventing a convention.
- [ISDA collateralized cash-price settlement practice](https://www.isda.org/2018/11/26/market-practice-change-for-settlement-of-eur-swaptions-to-collateralized-cash-price/) motivates an explicitly named alternative to older cash-settlement approximations; the implementation's delayed-cash carry policy is separately disclosed above.
- [ISDA settlement matrix](https://www.isda.org/book/isda-definitions-settlement-matrix-for-early-termination-and-swaptions/) reinforces that legal settlement method is a declared contract choice. Full matrix replication is not claimed.
- [QuantLib rate-curve material](https://www.quantlib.org/slides/rate-curves.pdf) describes distinct discounting and forwarding curves. Exact supplied factors are used here; no bootstrapping is added.
- [BCBS counterparty-credit-risk guidelines](https://www.bis.org/bcbs/publ/d588.pdf) motivates reviewed legal netting, collateral operational risk, exposure and counterparty-loss separation. This is not regulatory compliance or an automatic capital engine.
- [Bilateral closeout research](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.3355) identifies replacement closeout and settlement conventions as material choices. This component limits own-default and requires an explicitly reviewed MPOR obligation/exercise policy rather than inferring it.
