# Native observed-panel credit calibration

`portfolio_model_core::credit_snapshot_calibration::fit(&SnapshotCalibrationRequest)` implements `credit-snapshot-calibration-1`. It estimates a declared bounded CTMC from individual interval-censored state snapshots using endpoint transition probabilities. It complements `observed-credit-calibration-1`, which requires exact transition times and exposure spells. Neither contract invents event times from monthly tapes. Python adapters transport raw observations and format results; likelihood, optimization, transition math and diagnostics execute in Rust.

## Observation and identity contract

Each snapshot supplies unique record/account/raw-source IDs, exact observation timestamps, observed origin/end states, named covariates frozen over the interval and a declared term level. Timestamps are ordinal dates plus seconds-of-day and use ACT/365 elapsed seconds. Intervals are within study windows and last (0,1] years. For an account, intervals are contiguous, nonoverlapping and endpoint states match. States are assumed observed without misclassification; observation schedules, entry and end of observation are assumed noninformative conditional on the supplied model. Missing covariates, unknown states/term levels, gaps and informative observation mechanisms are unsupported and require a separately declared model. A final known live snapshot contributes its endpoint likelihood without inventing subsequent exposure.

Mandatory request identities include source/revision, population/model policy, distinct training/holdout split IDs and `data_kind` (`observed` or `synthetic`). Ordinal windows obey `train_start < train_end < holdout_end`; the full study is <=100 years. All monetary outcome fields share mandatory `monetary_unit`; the unit is retained with no conversion or implicit multiplier. Individual record/account/source/partition provenance is returned. Marking a request observed is a caller declaration, not automatic evidence authentication.

Intervals ending exactly at `train_end` belong to training; intervals starting exactly there belong to holdout. An interval crossing the split has no observed boundary state and is rejected or excluded with its ID/diagnostic, according to explicit policy. It is not divided into invented state spells. Hidden-state propagation through a chronological split is a different model contract and is not silently inferred. The optimizer and observed-information calculation receive training cases only. Holdout changes affect diagnostics and never parameter search, active bounds or information.

## Declared hazard family and likelihood

Each performing/watch/delinquent row is either an explicit fixed row carrying assumption ID/rationale or a list of enabled log-linear edges. Omitted edges are declared structural zeros; diagonals are forbidden. For enabled edge i->j:

`q_ij(x,k) = exp(intercept_ij + sum_r beta_ijr*x_r + term_effect_ijk)`.

Term level 0 is the reference with exactly zero effect. The caller provides initial/lower/upper coefficients, named covariates and term levels; the engine does not choose drivers, center observations using holdout, or synthesize macro series. Candidate outgoing intensities must sum to <=100/year; excessive rows are infeasible, never capped. Covariates are in [-10,10], coefficients in [-20,20], and all supplied bounds are explicit. A log-linear origin with no observed training-start intervals is rejected and requires an explicit supplied fixed row. This conservative admission rule also avoids silently labeling evidence-free row coefficients as observed estimates.

For an interval with generator Q and elapsed time dt, likelihood contribution is `P_ij(dt)` where `P=exp(Q*dt)`. Default probability sums the three internal default-origin columns of the shared seven-state operator; prepay uses its separate absorbing column. Thus observed performing->performing includes unobserved migrations and cures, and observed performing->default integrates every compatible latent path. The native objective sums `-log(P_ij)` over training records, conditional on each observed starting state. This is not transition-count/exposure MLE, nor an annual cumulative-PD-to-hazard shortcut. Identical interval/covariate/term cases are grouped with endpoint counts as sufficient numerical work reduction, preserving observation provenance separately.

[Jackson's primary panel-model paper](https://www.jstatsoft.org/article/view/v038i08) establishes the distinction between observed endpoints and unknown intermediate paths. [The author's model documentation](https://chjackson.github.io/msm/reference/msm.html) distinguishes panel observations from exact transitions and describes log-linear, piecewise covariate intensities. Those principles motivate this bounded schema; misclassified states, unknown terminal state, arbitrary mid-interval coefficient switches and general hidden-state models are not implemented here.

## Optimizer, convergence and identifiability

The deterministic native optimizer explores negative/positive coordinate directions within supplied coefficient bounds, accepts objective improvements exceeding the declared tolerance, and halves an unsuccessful mesh until the declared final mesh. It returns `coordinate_mesh_converged` only after a complete unsuccessful sweep at that mesh; interrupted/budget-limited sweeps return `search_budget_exhausted`. No derivatives, Python callbacks, external solver or randomness enter the search. `parameters` contains final estimates with stable origin/edge/covariate/term names; `declared_rows` retains original supplied templates and initial values. The latter must not be mistaken for fitted coefficients.

Coordinate-mesh convergence is a local numerical stopping criterion, not a proof of a unique/global statistical optimum. [Torczon's primary pattern-search paper](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/S1052623493250780) is background on direct-search methods; this finite, constrained implementation does not claim all of that paper's theoretical convergence conditions. Independent starts, competing plausible model specifications and portfolio validation remain review duties. Coefficients near supplied bounds are listed and prevent adoption eligibility. A structural zero/no-event origin should use a reviewed explicit policy rather than a positive log-hazard forced against an arbitrary lower bound.

For an interior converged candidate, central training-objective differences with coefficient step 1e-3 construct the observed-information matrix. A bounded symmetric Jacobi decomposition reports its eigenvalues. `information_rank` counts positive eigenvalues exceeding `1e-5 * max(1,max_abs_eigenvalue)`. Missing information, negative/near-zero curvature or collinear/unobserved covariate effects prevent full rank. This finite-difference rank is a numerical identifiability diagnostic, not a mathematical proof, a confidence interval, or uncertainty calibration. No confidence interval is invented from an ill-conditioned inverse.

`adoption_eligible` requires observed caller data, mesh convergence, no active coefficient bounds and full numerical information rank. Synthetic requests always return false. Eligibility is a numerical admission result requiring model review, not empirical accuracy certification, accounting-policy approval or automatic activation. Missing observed bank histories keeps empirical issue gates open. Fixed supplied rows retain their assumption tags even when other origins are observed fits. All outputs declare `physical` measure; no observed-frequency fit is relabeled as a pricing measure or converted to it.

Holdout reports log likelihood, observed start/endpoint counts, predicted endpoint counts, impossible intervals, and rows outside the admitted hazard domain. Impossible events have null overall log likelihood, not an arbitrary epsilon probability. Domain-rejected cases are counted; their predictions are absent from numeric sums rather than fabricated. Holdout evaluation is separate from model selection and calibration. The diagnostics do not implement full censoring-adjusted calibration curves, bootstrap parameter uncertainty, subgroup fairness or causal driver effects.

## Observed severity and exposure outcomes

`SnapshotCalibrationResult::project_hazards(covariates, term_level)` evaluates final named coefficients in Rust, validates shape/identity/bounds and rejects out-of-domain projected rows. It is available for explicitly synthetic diagnostics too. It does not activate a model, authenticate observation provenance or imply that an ineligible estimate passed empirical review. Consumers must inspect the gates and retain model/source identities before any separately reviewed adoption.

Optional default outcomes supply unique account/outcome/raw-source IDs, exactly observed last live default origin, default timestamp, immediately-before-default drawn/undrawn, realized principal EAD, complete dated nominal principal recoveries, and an explicit complete resolution or right-censor timestamp. EAD must be between drawn and drawn+undrawn. Interest, costs, discounts, principal modification and product transformation are not silently inserted into these principal-only fields. An account has at most one absorbing default outcome in this study; repeated cured defaults need a richer explicit default-state contract.

Resolved training defaults enter training severity only when default **and full resolution** occur by train end. A training default resolved later is excluded with a distinct provenance reason, preventing future recovery leakage into historical training. Defaults strictly after the split with complete resolution by holdout end enter holdout diagnostics. Right-censored recovery processes require explicit reject or exclude-with-diagnostics policy; no unresolved remainder is inferred to be zero. Complete rows return pooled and exact-default-origin versions of:

`realized LGD ratio = sum(EAD - nominal recovered principal) / sum(EAD)`;

`realized CCF ratio = sum(EAD - immediately-before-default drawn) / sum(immediately-before-default undrawn)`.

Zero denominators return null. These are explicitly EAD-weighted/undrawn-weighted complete-case realized ratios, not regulatory long-run default-weighted LGD, a discounted workout estimate, a covariate severity regression or a downturn calibration. [The Basel Committee's working paper on validation](https://www.bis.org/publ/bcbs_wp14.pdf) discusses incomplete workouts and inconsistent recovery data; excluding unresolved cases can bias the remaining sample. [The current BIS credit-risk guidance](https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/basel-consolidated-guidelines/module/cri/30) distinguishes observed losses from regulatory parameters and economic recovery costs/discounting. The module exposes exclusions and raw IDs, and makes no Basel/CECL/IFRS9 compliance claim. Portfolio-representative censoring treatment, observed recovery timing/survival fits and justified richer EAD/LGD driver models remain separate empirical gates.

## Limits and independent evidence

At most 20,000 snapshot records, 20,000 default outcomes, four covariates, four term levels, twelve free parameters, and 512 distinct interval/covariate/term groups per split are admitted. Each default has <=4,096 recovery entries; aggregate recovery entries <=200,000. Monetary states/totals are <=1e15 and metadata <=32 MiB, with control-free 1..256-byte labels. Search evaluations are 1..2,000; total likelihood/diagnostic evaluations <=2,400, including information differences. Uniformization work is conservatively charged as 128 terms times bounded chunks for each group/evaluation and limited to 10,000,000. Group bounds and actual work checks remain component limits, not an aggregate memory or 60,000-account fitting acceptance claim. Failures produce no partial accepted result.

Independent fixtures prove a one-edge panel MLE `q=-ln(3/4)` for three survivors and one default in a one-year interval; separate covariate and term effects; performing/watch cure endpoint probabilities `2/3 + exp(-3)/3`; train-end inclusion; unchanged parameters/information when only holdout endpoints change; explicit split/censor exclusions; null unsupported severity denominators; and singular/boundary/nonconverged rejection gates. [The small synthetic JSON example](../../examples/models/credit-snapshot-calibration-1.json) is a numerical demonstration with no empirical accuracy claim. Native product/graph/daily adoption, observed representative datasets, external benchmark estimates, independent uncertainty/holdout review and durable publication remain necessary before #11 is considered complete.
