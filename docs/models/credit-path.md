# Native exact event-time credit paths

`portfolio_model_core::credit_path::evaluate(&CreditPathRequest)` implements `credit-path-1`. It samples the same performing/watch/delinquent/default/prepaid continuous-time state family as the deterministic operator. It retains migrations, cures, default origin, competing exits, earned interest, realized exposure, supplied contractual boundaries and full recovery tails. It does not turn the expected-exposure ledger into a stochastic ledger automatically. Legacy requests retain their existing model selection and economics.

## Contract and units

Every request declares `measure` (`physical` or `pricing`), `monetary_unit`, immutable source/revision/model policy IDs, an `origin_time` ordinal plus seconds-of-day, the complete piecewise supplied path, and path IDs/unique numerical CRN keys. The engine preserves the measure; it does not convert physical estimates into pricing hazards. Money is the actual amount of a single realized account. There is no survival/probability multiplier in the monetary state and no second default haircut. The caller aggregates paths and supplies any sampling weights outside this single-account contract.

Times are elapsed ACT/365 years from `origin_time`: one year is exactly 31,536,000 elapsed seconds. Exact event offsets remain `f64` and are not rounded to days or 30-day months. The origin timestamp is retained. Negative interest receivables/coupon accrual and signed carrying premiums/discounts are supported. Principal and undrawn commitment are nonnegative. Principal, undrawn, absolute accrued interest and absolute basis together are bounded by the shared 1e15 currency-amount limit per path state; closing aggregate counters and each origin's earned interest have that limit too. No currency conversion or implicit thousands multiplier occurs.

Each interval has a strictly increasing `end_year`, a 3 by 5 matrix of annual **continuous intensities**, signed annual simple `accrual_rates`, and per-live-origin LGD, CCF, interest recovery fraction, and a complete recovery lag/weight distribution. Diagonal hazards must be exactly zero; outgoing intensities sum to at most 100/year. LGD/CCF/recovery fractions lie in [0,1]. Interval coefficients are frozen over that interval. They are supplied assumptions unless independently supported and reviewed; annual cumulative PDs are not interpreted as intensities.

## Event algorithm and shared draws

For current live state i, let lambda_i = sum_j q_ij. Draw `E = -ln(U_wait)` and carry its unused internal waiting time across every term and monetary boundary. Consume `lambda_i * elapsed_years`; a jump occurs at the exact first time the accumulated intensity reaches E. Select its destination by `U_destination * lambda_i` and ordered cumulative destination intensities. A new pair is obtained after a jump; zero-intensity stretches consume no internal time. This handles default/prepay as mutually exclusive competing events and permits multiple migrations/cures before either absorbing exit. At equal times, credit transitions use the ending interval first, then contractual boundaries execute in supplied stable order, then the new interval applies. Events belong to the ending interval. Floating evaluation of a mathematically bounded jump time is limited to that interval endpoint; probabilities and hazards are never clipped or normalized.

The method is the direct event-time sampling construction applied to piecewise intensities. [Anderson's primary paper on integrated internal times](https://web.math.wisc.edu/~dfanderson/papers/AndNRM.pdf) explains why unused internal time can be retained when intensities change. [Vestergaard and Génois's temporal Gillespie paper](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pcbi.1004579) develops total-intensity timing for time-varying networks. Their mathematical methods inform this implementation; no biochemical or network calibration is imported into credit.

Two explicit draw owners are supported:

- `supplied`: exact key coverage, finite open-(0,1) waiting/destination pairs, versioned stream identity. Exhaustion is an error; no fallback occurs. Unused trailing pairs are allowed. A partially consumed final waiting pair counts in `random_pairs_used`.
- `counter_splitmix64_v1`: caller seed and stream identity, stable wrapping-u64 counter generation keyed by path key and zero-based jump index. The constants and mix sequence in `credit_path.rs` are the versioned reproducibility definition. Waiting and destination lanes use separate domains. A 52-bit midpoint map makes both uniforms strictly interior. The stream is noncryptographic and has no claim of optimal statistical coupling or cryptographic randomness. Stream labels identify the caller's run; changing labels without changing the seed does not change numeric draws.

Reordering accounts, adding another account, splitting an unchanged interval, or changing a path label preserves another path's numerical stream when its seed/key remain fixed. Scenario comparisons use the same key/seed or supplied pairs; term edits retain the residual internal wait. Different realized migrations can change later event indices. CRN does not promise identical destinations across scenario coefficients or minimum-variance competing-channel coupling. Cross-platform transcendental functions may produce last-bit differences; byte-for-byte determinism is tested within the same runtime.

## Monetary conservation and boundaries

During a live sojourn, `earned_interest = principal * origin_accrual_rate * elapsed_years` adds to signed accrued rights. The result retains total earned interest and its actual live-origin allocation. A cure changes the state/rate prospectively; previously earned or nonaccruing rights persist.

At default from origin i, contingent funded draw is `undrawn * CCF_i`, EAD is current principal plus that draw, and the funding cash is negative. Undrawn is canceled. Economic principal loss is `EAD * LGD_i`. For each complete recovery weight w at exact default time plus lag, principal recovery is `EAD * (1-LGD_i) * w` and interest recovery is `accrued_interest * interest_recovery_fraction_i * w`. All tail entries are returned even after the report horizon. Only report-horizon recoveries reduce closing claims and enter report cash. Default basis remains a signed right requiring a separate reviewed accounting policy; economic loss is not an allowance or a write-off instruction. Accrual stops at default. Claim amounts and source origin are retained even when the report ends before recovery.

At prepay, principal and signed accrued interest are settled once, signed book adjustment is recognized separately, and undrawn is canceled. A repayment boundary settles supplied state-dependent fractions of actual principal/interest, recognizes the same principal fraction of basis, and cancels the specified commitment fraction. A draw boundary funds a supplied fraction of actual remaining undrawn. `maturity` settles actual remaining principal/interest, recognizes basis, cancels commitment and returns a distinct `matured` outcome. Maturity is never recorded as prepayment. Default/prepaid/matured accounts no longer receive loan boundaries; default recovery rights continue independently.

The general principal cash check per path is opening principal plus funded draws equals principal repayments/prepayments/maturity cash, in-horizon principal recoveries, and closing live/default principal. Economic loss remains part of the gross default claim until a separate accounting owner applies explicit charge-off policy. Interest opening plus realized earned interest equals interest settlements/recoveries plus closing rights. Opening signed basis equals recognized basis plus remaining basis. The module emits economic flows and rights, not balanced GL, taxes, CECL/IFRS9 allowances or accounting compliance claims.

## Bounds and evidence

Requests admit 1..10,000 paths, 1..4,096 intervals, at most 4,096 ordered boundaries, 32 strictly increasing recovery lags per interval/origin, horizon <=100 years and lag <=100 years. Complete tail weights sum to one within 1e-12 and cannot exceed one. Maximum jumps per path is explicit; path count times the maximum is <=250,000. Retained jump/boundary events are <=250,000, supplied pairs <=260,000, and actual interval/boundary/recovery work <=2,000,000 units. Labels contain 1..256 control-free bytes. Violations return errors with no partial accepted result. These are component admission limits, not an aggregate RSS or combined portfolio-scale claim.

Independent fixtures use supplied uniforms with hand-computed integrated hazard, two-stage waiting time, actual-origin cure occupation, negative coupons, contingent draw and recovery tails. Tests cover semigroup-style interval refinement, counter stream account ordering, reproducibility, missing uniforms, schema/domain rejection and maturity separation. [The synthetic example](../../examples/models/credit-path-1.json) defaults at year 0.3, earns 3 interest, funds 10, and retains recovery 66 principal plus 3 interest at year 2.3 beyond its one-year horizon.

Remaining #11 gates include empirical portfolio validation, wider observed severity/term models where justified, adoption by every product/scenario graph and daily stochastic accounting publication, correlated borrower/common drivers, Monte Carlo uncertainty and reviewed accounting policy. This component does not close those gates or replace the legacy model implicitly.
