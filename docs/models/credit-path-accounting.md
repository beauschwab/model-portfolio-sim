# Realized credit path accounting

`credit-path-accounting-1` adopts a supplied, reproducible physical credit path into daily balanced journal intents. Rust owns event simulation, actual exposure accrual, credit rights, recovery settlement and accounting policy application. Python can transport and independently verify the immutable request and result. This module is an analytical adoption interface; it does not by itself constitute the complete saved-book streamed ledger, publication, capital or empirical model approval gates under issues #11/#12 and tracker #25.

The public Rust function is `credit_path_accounting::evaluate(&PathAccountingRequest)`. The request nests the strict `credit-path-1` contract, a required calendar convention and tolerance, a reporting end ordinal and one explicit `PathBinding` per realized path key. Missing bindings, duplicate keys/positions/claim IDs, unknown JSON fields, incompatible sources, pricing measure and non-normalized monetary units reject. The full economic source ID/revision must match the nested request; policy, recovery schedule and interest settlement reviews retain separate IDs and revisions. The synthetic complete example is [credit-path-accounting-1.json](../../examples/models/credit-path-accounting-1.json).

## Probability and money

Each path is one realized account. Its principal, undrawn commitment, recognized signed accrued right and signed book adjustment are absolute currency amounts. They never receive another survival or probability multiplier. Performing, watch and delinquent migrations transport the whole account; a cure can restore subsequent accrual under the supplied live-state rates. Monetary rights can default out of an origin with no opening exposure after a migration.

The daily probability vector is a separate one-hot diagnostic in the order performing, watch, delinquent, default-from-performing, default-from-watch, default-from-delinquent, prepaid. Maturity has its own `matured_probability`; it is not prepayment or default. Vector mass plus matured mass is checked against one without normalization or negative clipping. The path's economic outcome is retained even after accounting has collected or charged off all default rights.

Hazards, rates, LGD, CCF and recovery timings are supplied piecewise term inputs. The underlying [credit path](credit-path.md) consumes one integrated waiting threshold across term boundaries with stable common random number keys. The temporal Gillespie derivation provides a primary reference for integrated waiting hazards and competing event selection under changing rates ([Vestergaard and Génois, 2015](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1004579)). This is a numerical construction, not evidence that supplied credit parameters fit observed bank histories.

## Exact economic time and daily recognition

Elapsed years use ACT/365 elapsed seconds, with 31,536,000 seconds per year. Calendar ordinals and `seconds_of_day` identify the economic time. The required `calendar_midnight_right_closed` mapping recognizes events over `(previous midnight, reporting midnight]`. An exact midnight event belongs to that closing day; an intraday event belongs to the following midnight close. Reporting starts at the first midnight after the supplied origin. Opening-instant events at an origin midnight reject, because they require a separately specified opening accounting window. The path horizon must end at the explicit reporting midnight.

`timestamp_tolerance_seconds` must be explicitly supplied in 0 through one microsecond. It only resolves numerical ties to midnight; raw elapsed years/seconds remain in the output. It is not a business settlement lag. The result retains exact economic events, mapped recognition dates and complete recovery records, so daily recognition cannot be mistaken for simulated end-of-day default timing.

The additive `credit_path::evaluate_traced` API returns the same economic result as ordinary evaluation plus live exposure sojourns. This coordinator splits sojourns at calendar boundaries. For each piece `[s,e]`, earned interest is

`earned = actual_principal * supplied_origin_accrual_rate * (e - s)`.

Accrual raises the signed live right and posts the opposite interest-income entry. `TimedIntent.accrual` retains start/end years, origin, principal, rate and earned amount for independent replay. Accrual completes before an event at the same instant; original event ordering is retained for credit/boundary ties. No nonaccrual or cure catch-up income is inferred beyond the supplied rates. Interest received before earning, unrecognized contingent coupon rights and arbitrary liability products require another explicit contract rather than relabeling their amounts as earned interest here.

## Events and reviewed policy

Ordinary draws fund cash and reduce actual undrawn commitment. Scheduled repayments use explicit per-origin principal/interest/cancellation fractions. Prepayment and maturity settle principal, accrued rights and signed basis once; maturity remains a distinct boundary. Basis recognition posts a signed expense, so a discount produces income. Contractual policy names remain in `TimedIntent.contractual_policy_id` within the immutable economic source; the journal's policy identity is the separately reviewed accounting policy. No unrelated policy revision is invented.

Default captures the actual origin's principal, signed accrued right and basis, plus a supplied CCF-funded draw on actual undrawn commitment. The shared `credit_accounting` implementation applies that default instruction, contingent funding, reviewed accrual/basis policy, chargeoffs and principal recoveries. Economic principal loss is reported independently of allowance. It does not choose allowance, chargeoff dates or accounting treatment.

The original shared implementation was promoted to model-core; ledger-native's existing module is a pure re-export. Original policy choices and evaluation order remain available to existing ledger callers. The path coordinator applies a `before_credit` allowance target before shared default/chargeoff/principal recovery processing, then signed interest cash, then any `after_credit` target. This makes the latter target the closing reviewed amount after all credit cash. Reviews have dates rather than intraday timestamps. The shared daily order of reviewed chargeoffs before principal recovery is retained and disclosed; it is not presented as an inferred intraday chronology.

## Recovery rights and complete tails

Every generated recovery point has an identity derived from its bound claim and point index, with the supplied recovery review revision. Exact point times, signed interest amounts, principal amounts and `after_reporting` flags remain in `complete_recoveries`, including points beyond the reporting horizon. Principal points on the same recognition day are coalesced for the shared strict-date schedule, while the original IDs and exact times remain in journal lineage. Claims persist after principal exits and chargeoffs.

`credit_accounting::settle_interest_recoveries` is a separate atomic helper under `credit-interest-recovery-1`; it does not re-run principal collection. Positive receivables and negative coupon payables settle with correspondingly signed cash. `interest_recovery_applied` records the reduction of the remaining recognized right, while `interest_recovery_cash_collected` separately records physical cash. The conservation equation is

`initial_accrued = outstanding_accrued + accrued_reversed + interest_recovery_applied`.

Cash after a reviewed reversal or accrued chargeoff can exceed the remaining right; explicit policy credits the allowance or recognizes signed recovery income. A negative excess allowance movement must leave the allowance nonnegative. Amounts opposite to the original right, amounts exceeding the uncollected original right, mismatched dates/sources, unknown claims and duplicate recovery IDs reject. Settled IDs remain in the claim so replay cannot collect a point twice. These three additive claim fields deserialize to zero/empty for historical records, preserving legacy input compatibility.

LGD and recovery schedule validation do not establish empirical recovery accuracy. The [Basel Committee's validation study](https://www.bis.org/publications/studies-validation-internal-rating-systems-revised) discusses PD/LGD validation and highlights recovery-related validation questions. Complete tails here are supplied assumptions; observed estimation still requires appropriately dated resolved/censored workout histories and held-out validation. This interface makes no CECL, IFRS or regulatory compliance assertion.

## Conservation, output and admission

The result returns immutable source/measure/unit identity, economic paths, daily rows, balanced timed intents, complete mapped recoveries and shared closing accounting state. A debit-positive twelve-account book is replayed locally from principal/accrual/basis/allowance openings. Every intent balances; its actual GL account balances match independent closing live/default rights and allowance. Cash and earned interest reconcile to the realized economic path. Net assets including cash roll forward by cumulative profit. Final default rights reconcile to economic remaining rights after explicitly accounting for chargeoffs, reversals, excess recovered cash and recognized basis. No discarded tail, second survival weighting or forced mass normalization is used.

Admission is at most 256 bound paths, 36,600 reporting days, 250,000 daily rows, 250,000 retained intents, two million counted work units and 32 MiB charged metadata. Nested intervals/boundaries are at most 4,096 each and each origin's complete recovery tail has at most 32 points. All input tails are counted before trace evaluation; every realized mapped tail is checked and charged before per-path allocations, including dates beyond the report. Derived recovery IDs must also fit the 256-byte control-free identity limit. Currency amounts are finite and bounded by shared `MAX_MONETARY_AMOUNT` (1e15). Signed accrued/basis amounts are admitted explicitly; principal and undrawn remain nonnegative and principal plus basis cannot be negative. These component admissions are not an aggregate process RSS guarantee. Failed evaluation returns no partial result.

Independent Rust fixtures cover exact half-day default, contingent draw, positive/negative interest, reviewed chargeoff, default reversal and excess cash, after-credit allowance ordering, migration into an initially empty default origin, zero-credit repayment/maturity, same-day coalescing with exact lineage, long recovery tails, trace/economic identity and malformed domains. The canonical synthetic example has opening principal 100, undrawn 20, basis -3 and allowance 41; it earns 0.05 before default, funds 10, receives principal recovery 66 plus interest 0.05, then charges off principal 44 and basis -3 against allowance 41. Closing cash is 56.05 and cumulative profit 0.05. Synthetic hand cases validate mechanics only; they do not close empirical calibration or complete integrated ledger acceptance gates.
