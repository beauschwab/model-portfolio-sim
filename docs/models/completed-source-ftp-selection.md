# Completed modeled-product sources for FTP

The Capital & FTP panel can select one or two completed Card2 mixed or performing-deposit source1 jobs. Raw modeled-product inputs remain available in the existing mode. The selected-source route reconstructs each retained raw source and independently verifies its complete source and journal under the new job deadline; saved verification flags are evidence to reproduce, not permission.

Choose the source kind and completed job ID, then check its metadata. The helper reads bounded authoritative job/request metadata twice and returns owner/run/path indices and labels. It neither loads full journals nor executes financial calculations. Editing an ID or kind invalidates the checked selection. Journal verification, regulatory eligibility and accounting approval remain false on that metadata response.

Supply an explicit source alias, scope-review ID/revision and a complete reviewed funding policy. Policy indexed reviews must identify each declared path, and aliases must match its charges. Omit `source.sources` in this mode. Curves, repricing/liquidity tenor policies, cash assignments and contingent/option charges remain supplied inputs. The helper provides no financial or regulatory defaults. Card2 scope funds exclusive product owners while retaining inherited dated sources without funding them; deposit scope covers the isolated performing owner. Portfolio source2 is not yet a completed-source kind.

The new native preparation owns funding coefficients and FTP math. Source reproduction and immutable Parquet replay are publication gates. The panel waits through bounded job status, then pages reports and supports reopening/download; it never hydrates full result or journal manifests. The private source binding rechecks exact durable row, queued raw input, object hashes, current source/native identity, original deadline, cancellation, lease and token at publication. Failed publication produces no successful report.

Declared typed Rust DTO financial fields normalize integer spelling to binary64 only at their documented clone boundary. Raw `serde_json::Value` specifications retain integer/float/bool and signed-zero identity exactly. Standalone raw input identity and nested typed source clone identity are checked separately before computed financial comparisons. Financial tolerances are unchanged. Actual stored ObjectReference nodes are decoded only to bounded descriptors; contents remain hashchecked by their reader. Card2 frame reads admit encoded and logical decoded components separately with cooperative cancellation before Polars materialization; these component bounds are not an aggregate RSS guarantee.

This is reviewed synthetic/source-linked FTP preparation, not empirical calibration, accounting-standard approval, regulatory eligibility or full saved-book/cohort/60,000-position adoption. Capital source eligibility remains distinct. Existing manual, legacy bridge and raw synthetic modes retain their contracts.
