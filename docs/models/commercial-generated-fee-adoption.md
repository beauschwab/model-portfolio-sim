## Joint stopping ownership

Let `L(t)` be the loan discount factor including the retained original loan OAS
and selected paired bump, and `F(t)` the separate fee discount factor including
its explicit spread and the same bump. At a decision date, the common loan
numeraire target is

`loan continuation + F(t)/L(t) * fee continuation`.

Equivalently every future fee amount contributes `cash(u)*F(u)/L(t)`. A next-date
joint value already expressed in loan units propagates by `L(next)/L(t)`.
Adding separately rebased loan/fee values without that ratio is forbidden.
Native hypothetical stopped loan carries bind the actual fee exit policy and
complete tail before computing each action payoff. Appending fee cash after a
loan-only decision is not this contract.

Only current observable loan state, published fixings and fee states enter the
regression. Eleven service state coordinates per fee retain earned/refunded,
canceled, waived/recovered, net cash, gross receivable/payable, deferred/prepaid
and unearned entitlement. Physical acquired cost and cost-release balances are
not Pricing features. Native prefix selection respects before/after scheduled
cash. Future credit outcomes and future fee groups are excluded from features.
The existing64-feature and minimum-live-training-column limits remain in force.

Training and fresh evaluation retain separate declared streams. Original and
paired scenario Pricing laws retain the same CRN rules. Physical laws apply
the sealed corresponding Pricing policy; they do not refit it. A Physical
observable state absent from training rejects rather than extrapolating a
constant feature. Fee contract compatibility is checked before generation.

Original loan OAS calibration, when selected, must refit this joint policy at
each trial while matching **loan-component** stopped PV to the original loan
quote. Fee spread stays fixed, and the separately computed final fee PV must
match its own original quote. Acquiring the sum of two prices does not merge
their accounting yields. Historical fee-omitted calibration stays unchanged.

sealed original acquisition context. They do not retain128 expanded fee owners.
The original fee quote is checked on original stopped Pricing cash; complete
original Physical policies and reviewed signed cost releases remain separate.

The stream expands one actual Physical loan/fee source pair, constructs compact
sealed journal sources, and drops that expanded pair before the next path.
Compact fee groups retain authoritative atomic postings and absolute gross
states, not endpoints reconstructed by repeatedly summing deltas. Ordinary
borrowed fee owners preserve their existing behavior. Both owners enter the
same Sim ordering: scenario/day/native year, loan or fee kind, binding and group.
Allocation face/source weights apply once in the existing Binding factor.

Full journal horizon includes supplied fee-only tails, acquisition release
tails, loan rights and recovery tails. No settlement, release, cash or terminal
zero balance is inferred from a missing event. The new transport uses the same
bounded immutable partition storage and SQL-backed independent replay spool.
Final API publication retains original raw input, executable/source identities,
deadline, cancellation, job ownership and private final SQL authority fences.

Work inventory must follow actual call counts: policy admission may validate a
fee owner before source generation; each path binds its un-stopped fee source,
each available action/date compiles a genuine stopped loan/fee owner, fitting
and evaluation traverse their separate samples, selected source replay binds
the actual stopped fee, Physical acquisition adds reviewed cost events, and
journal materialization binds the final paired source. These are additional
costs, not free receipt construction. Hypothetical stop owners are sequential,
but the live prefix/point/fee state and its current stop owner coexist and are
charged together. Original/current compact recipes coexist across roles and
cannot be treated as disjoint peak phases.

Capacity may use a maximum only for proven sequential owner lifetimes. Fee
state features increase the existing selected basis dimension and cannot be
excluded merely because a fixture happens to make them constant. Native
realized active-column coverage still validates the actual fit. Oversized
events, fee counts, tails, role sets, bases or source bytes reject; no cap,
sample minimum or final financial tolerance is relaxed.

## File ownership and shared seams

New generated-specific Rust helpers own fee law validation, joint targets,
observed features, stopped fee cash and compact economic recipes. Narrow
stochastic/generated policy/graph/unit/candidate wiring selects them. Ledger
fee binding gains a sealed borrowed-or-compact source interface; original
borrowed groups and serialized omission remain exact. A new compact fee
adapter owns interning and absolute snapshots.

The finite fee worker owns transfer/principal/acquisition/factor owners. Shared
fee binding already exposes crate-local `bind_carry`. A narrow generated
adapter consumes an already bound fee path through the existing validated
projection/acquired-cost logic after coordination, without fabricating an
incomplete finite Selection or rerunning a different loan owner.

New independent generated-fee reference code recomputes raw fee sources,
training-only policies, cash, gross endpoints and signed cost releases. Raw
graph/unit checks and ordered/persisted fee journal checks consume one expanded
path at a time, with expected transactions and coverage on the bounded SQLite
spool. Native metadata and caller receipts never confer financial authority.
