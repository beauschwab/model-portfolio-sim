# Reviewed performing-deposit portfolio FTP source

The explicit `deposit_portfolio` source kind selects the complete
`deposit-performing-portfolio-ledger-2` owner and the separately bounded
`modeled-product-ftp-source-channels-2` funding tier. It does not relabel or change
historical `deposit_performing` or Card2 source behavior. Funding and charge
formulas are common to both tiers; the additional tier admits complete longer
histories and multiple exclusive owners only when their joined work and capacity
reservations fit.

## Inputs and economic ownership

Select compatible completed performing-deposit portfolio jobs in Capital & FTP,
check their bounded metadata, and supply a source alias, scope-review identity,
complete indexed owner/run/path reviews and the full reviewed funding policy.
The whole-portfolio scope is `whole_performing_deposit_portfolio-2`; path index is
zero for each supplied owner/run. Metadata checking establishes compatibility,
never permission, regulatory eligibility, funding assumptions or empirical fit.
The raw synthetic-example mode remains available separately.

The source retains supplied original cost, principal and original yield anchor,
complete original and revised physical histories, observed prefix, paid-rate lag,
revision policy, actual native principal atoms and gross FIFO earned/cash origins.
The actual source journals are independently regenerated and replayed before FTP
publication. No new draw, inflow, migration, unknown prefix, seasoned-anchor,
negative-EIR or credit policy is inferred. Historical source-domain refusals remain.

Funding is built from native source-owned daily principal, signed basis and gross
rights. Actual cash is retained with explicit exclusion from a second funding
roll-forward. Original-EIR earnings and reviewed expectation-revision P&L have
separate labels and user-supplied inclusion rules. Rate-reset/reference tenor stays separate from behavioral funding/liquidity life.
Contingent, option, collateral and haircut inputs require their own explicit
reviewed policies; no new draw or collateral model is supplied by this deposit
route. If reviewed FTP and stress behavior differ, that difference must be
retained and disclosed. Internal business and treasury transfers offset; they do
not create external NII or regulatory capital.

A completed job ID is lineage, not an economic owner identifier. New-tier sources
require explicit raw account entity/currency identities and must have disjoint
`(entity, currency, canonical position ID)` identities across
selected portfolio jobs, even if their accounts or source aliases differ. Every
original/current owner/run tail is checked before participation; job selection
cannot omit an unused run or a gross origin to gain admission.

## Bounded native admission

The new primitive tier has a 365-day shape ceiling, 368 origins per state, 366
resets, 8M preparation work and 8MiB per primitive input/output. These are ceilings,
not a guarantee that every tuple fits. The source compiler independently retains
8M joined work and 32MiB component capacity; the outer source adapter retains
48M/256MiB and the complete FTP coordinator 62M/384MiB, with a 32MiB output limit.
All complete source histories, output staging and reviewed paths are charged
before source generation. Historical tier1 keeps its 64-day/2M/1MiB contracts.

For horizon H, one-segment no-inflow/no-migration ownership has I<=H earned origins
and C<=3H+1 cash events. Counted FIFO creates at most I+C fragments. The conversion
indexes those fragments once, retains native subtraction order and emits all
applicable gross origins. Conservative conversion work includes the historical
`state_count*18*256`, plus `(H+1)*(H+3)*96` and
`(H+1)*review_count*64` per review. Each portfolio source additionally reserves
16 work units and 10 capacity bytes per serialized typed-request byte for bounded
serialization/reparse staging. This restores the standalone selected Rust DTO
allocation boundary without changing raw identities or financial comparisons.
The existing active deadline remains in force; no nested Control or renewed
budget is created. Component capacity is not an aggregate RSS guarantee.

## Publication and remaining gates

The completed-source binder rechecks exact queued raw and declared Rust DTO
clones, current source/object descriptors, complete source journals, actual
producer completion and bounded Parquet frames. The new FTP job retains its
original accepted deadline and private authority through upload/reopen. Its
final SQLite guard rechecks queued request, current code/native bundle, source
objects, STOP, lease and token within the publication transaction. Calculation
receipts alone cannot grant permission or substitute for these gates.

The contained proposal demonstrates two-owner/two-run 40-day and one-owner/two-run
90-day cases through actual workers, the selector, native FTP, persisted replay,
private SQL, paged tables and reopen; corresponding late SQL refusals publish no
result. It is not installed-default acceptance, PostgreSQL validation, empirical
calibration or complete #14/#24 adoption. Full joint 60k scale, broader products,
saved-book/cohort mapping and all empirical gates remain required. Accounting
standard approval and regulatory permissions remain explicit and ungranted.
