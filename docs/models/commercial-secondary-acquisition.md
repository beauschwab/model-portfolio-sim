## Primary sources and regime boundary

[FASB ASU 2025-08, November 2025](https://storage.fasb.org/ASU%202025-08.pdf), printed pages 3 and 6, sets annual periods beginning after December 15, 2026, prospective acquisition application and permitted early adoption. Amended 310-10-35-53B separates credit discount from interest income. The update communicates amendments; the ASC is authoritative. This tier infers neither applicability/adoption nor initial credit gross-up from elapsed days. PCD/seasoned regimes require additional supported ownership and explicit policy.

[IFRS 9 overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-9-financial-instruments/) and IFRS 9 B5.4.1-B5.4.3 distinguish integral effective-interest fees and separately provided service fees. Classification remains supplied review. [IFRS 15 paragraphs 105-108](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/pdf-standards/english/2024/issued/part-a/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers.pdf?bypass=on) distinguishes unconditional receivables, contract assets and contract liabilities; it does not approve this model policy.

[TreasuryDirect purchase guidance](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/marketable-securities/buying-a-marketable-security/) illustrates purchased accrued interest returned with a subsequent coupon. That illustration supplies no legal commercial-loan conveyance evidence.
