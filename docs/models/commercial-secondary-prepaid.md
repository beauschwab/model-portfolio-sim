## Primary rationale



[IFRS15 paragraph55](https://www.ifrs.org/content/dam/ifrs/publications/pdf-standards/english/2022/issued/part-a/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers.pdf?bypass=on) separates expected refundable customer consideration from revenue entitlement; [paragraphs105–108](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/aasb-15-dec-2022) distinguish contract liabilities and receivables. These support retaining actual gross refund and service stocks separately from interparty purchase consideration. They do not authorize a legal netting arrangement, contractual service cancellation or this acquired residual release convention. Those are explicit supplied reviewed policies here. IFRS15 became mandatory for annual periods beginning1January2018 ([IFRS Foundation overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/)); publication edition dates are not entity-specific adoption evidence. Integral effective-interest fees remain governed by their separate reviewed classification and do not enter this service-fee lane automatically.
